## 📲 How to Add to Home Screen:

### iPhone (Safari):
1. Open Safari
2. Go to: `http://192.168.99.64:8000`
3. Tap **Share** button (bottom)
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **"Add"**
6. ✅ Done!

### Android (Chrome):
1. Open Chrome
2. Go to: `http://192.168.99.64:8000`
3. Tap menu (⋮) → **"Install app"**
4. Tap **"Install"**
5. ✅ Done!
