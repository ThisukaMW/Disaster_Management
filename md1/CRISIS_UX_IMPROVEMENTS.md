## 🎨 Visual Design Principles

### For Crisis Situations:
1. **High Contrast** - Easy to see in rain/wind
2. **Large Text** - 18-28px for readability
3. **Bold Fonts** - 600-700 weight
4. **Clear Colors** - Green (good), Red (warning), Orange (pending)
5. **Prominent Indicators** - Banners, borders, shadows
6. **Simple Animations** - Pulse for attention, slide for feedback
7. **No Fancy Effects** - Focus on usability, not decoration
